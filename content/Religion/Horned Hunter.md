## History
The [[Vila]] claimed that they were descendants of the **Horned Hunter** and worshiped him as their patron god.
After the [[Downfall of The Vila]] both the [[Nochni]] amd [[Shumani]] inherited reverence for the **Horned Hunter**. But as the [[Nochni]] got acclimated to living under **Imperial** rule they moved away from the worship of the **Horned Hunter**, buy the region of the [[Fifth Emperor]] the [[Nochni]] have mostly convrted to the [[Church of The Holy Martyr]].
