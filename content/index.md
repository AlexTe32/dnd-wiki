# The World, in Brief

One empire has outlasted every kingdom that tried to replace it. It is older than the maps that claim to chart it, old enough that its laws have laws, and its reach touches nearly every region worth naming — a governor here, a garrison there, a tax collector who arrives before the roads do. Steel still settles arguments the law can't reach, but almost everything, eventually, answers to the Empire.

An empire that size cannot be everywhere at once, and it knows it. What it can't govern directly, it pays for — a contract, a bounty, a writ with an official seal and an unofficial understanding that no one asks how the job got done. That's where you come in. Somewhere, right now, an imperial agent needs a ruin cleared, a debt collected, a monster put down, or a problem made to disappear quietly. It rarely matters which corner of the world you're standing in — the Empire's need for hired hands doesn't run out.

No one at your table needs to know the Empire's whole history to sit down and play in it. What's ancient history to a magistrate in one province is still an open wound in another, and different tables sit at different edges of the same map, in different years, working different contracts. You'll learn what's put a blade, a curse, or a bounty in front of _your_ character — the rest gets told as it comes up.
